I am **Henrietta Boowuo**, a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer and Information Technology at Purdue University’s School of Applied and Creative Computing. My research examines the human and organizational dimensions of AI-enabled information systems, focusing on **AI ethics and organizational governance, Human-centered AI, AI Safety and Security, Responsible Technology Adoption, and Accessibility**. I study how organizations adopt and govern emerging technologies, how people interact with AI and security systems, and how the risks and benefits of these technologies are distributed across users and organizations. My work addresses issues including fairness, accountability, accessibility, privacy, security, human oversight, organizational preparedness, and technology adoption, with applications spanning higher education, digital environments, and organizational settings. Through my research, teaching, and service, I seek to advance responsible and human-centered approaches to technology that help organizations realize the benefits of AI while supporting security, accessibility, meaningful human participation, and responsible decision-making.
