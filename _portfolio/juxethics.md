## Overview

**JuxEthics** is a web-based AI Ethics and Governance Assessment Tool that analyzes publicly available organizational disclosures across eight dimensions of responsible AI. It generates dimension-level scores, an overall assessment score, and disclosure classifications, supported by interactive dashboards, comparative assessments, and downloadable reports. The tool serves a dual purpose: **enabling organizations to assess their own publicly disclosed AI governance commitments and allowing external stakeholders to independently evaluate and compare organizational disclosures**. It supports the identification of disclosure gaps, informed decision-making, and research on AI governance transparency and accountability. Assessments are based on publicly available evidence rather than independently verified internal governance practices or regulatory compliance.

## Research Significance
As AI technologies become increasingly integrated into organizational operations and decision-making, assessing the transparency and accountability of organizational AI governance has become an important challenge. Although organizations increasingly publish responsible AI principles, policies, and commitments, these disclosures often vary in detail, consistency, and accessibility, making systematic evaluation and comparison difficult. JuxEthics addresses this gap by translating publicly available AI governance disclosures into structured, comparable indicators across eight dimensions of responsible AI. By identifying disclosure gaps and enabling cross-organizational comparisons, the tool supports organizational self-assessment, stakeholder decision-making, and research on AI governance transparency and accountability. Its contribution lies in providing a systematic approach to evaluating publicly documented governance commitments while distinguishing disclosure evidence from independently verified governance practices.

## Connection to Current Research
JuxEthics directly advances my research on institutional AI governance, transparency, and accountability, particularly my interest in examining how organizations communicate responsible AI commitments and how these commitments can be systematically assessed. The tool operationalizes ethical and governance principles into measurable disclosure indicators, providing a foundation for investigating differences in organizational AI governance transparency. It also supports my future research on whether publicly disclosed AI commitments align with actual governance practices, including human oversight, risk management, and accountability mechanisms. Building on JuxEthics, I aim to develop and validate assessment methods that integrate disclosure analysis with organizational evidence, stakeholder perspectives, and comparative case studies. This work will contribute to evidence-based approaches for evaluating institutional AI governance and strengthening accountability in responsible AI adoption.
